## Ontario threshold flags (auto-checked by HR agents)
- 20+ employees → AODA accessibility policy + training obligations scale up
- 25+ employees (Jan 1 count) → written **disconnecting-from-work** and
  **electronic-monitoring** policies required
- 50+ employees → Joint Health & Safety Committee required (OHSA)
- Ontario payroll ≥ $2.5M → ESA **severance pay** obligations apply
